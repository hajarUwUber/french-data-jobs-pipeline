# Business Need

## Persona
Moroccan graduates in France, under residency permit pressure,
searching for their first Data job before expiration.

## Pain point
Job search is vague and time-consuming: too many offers to sift through,
no clear view of profile ↔ offer compatibility.

## Enabled decisions
- Apply or not to a given offer
- Which skill to learn as a priority
- Which cities / companies to target

## Perfect insight
User profile ↔ offers matching, with prioritization:
high compatibility / medium / unlocked through learning.
